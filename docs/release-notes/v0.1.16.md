Bema 0.1.16 puts a quick prompt on the start screen.

### New
- **Quick prompt.** Bema's start screen has a box to paste any text: **Prompt this text** (⌘↩) opens the prompter with it, no deck needed. It shows how long your text is at a speaking pace.
- **Right-click the menu bar icon** to open Bema's panel, same as a left click.

### Fixed
- The box for your own text in the detached prompter scrolls again.

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
