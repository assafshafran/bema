Bema 0.1.5 makes the current slide resizable for real.

### Fixed
- **Drag the handle under the current slide to make it bigger or smaller.** In 0.1.3 and 0.1.4 dragging it did nothing you could see. The current slide now starts at two thirds of the column, and double-clicking the handle resets it. To make it wider, drag the divider between the slides and the notes.

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
