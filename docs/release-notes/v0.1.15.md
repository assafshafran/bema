Bema 0.1.15 lets you use the prompter without a presentation.

### New
- **Prompt your own text.** Paste or type anything into the detached prompter and prompt it with Voice, Auto or Manual, mirrored, under the camera and hidden from screen sharing, with no deck open. Find it in the menu bar (**Prompter for your own text**), **Slideshow → Prompter for Your Own Text…**, or `bema://prompter?text=…`. **Text** in the prompter's controls edits it or goes back to the deck's notes.

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
