Bema 0.1.8 lets you edit your speaker notes right in Bema.

### New
- **Edit notes in Bema.** Click the pencil next to a slide's title to change its notes, or **Type notes** on a slide that has none. Press ⌘↩ to keep the change or Esc to cancel.
- Your changes are marked **Edited**, and the prompter follows them straight away. Click **Save to deck** to write them into PowerPoint or Keynote, or ↶ to go back to the deck's notes.

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
