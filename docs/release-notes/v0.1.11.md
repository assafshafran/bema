Bema 0.1.11 makes the PowerPoint button one click and tidies the presenter window.

### Changed
- **Present with Bema in PowerPoint no longer asks "add-in wants to open Bema.app".** You'll see *Opening Bema…* for a moment, then Bema presents your deck. It uses the Accessibility permission Bema already has; without it, the button works as before.
- **The top bar fits narrow windows.** The LIVE badge and button captions no longer wrap or get cut off; when space runs out, the camera and Stage tools buttons show just their icons.

### Fixed
- Slide pictures could show an older version of a deck (the notes were right, the slides weren't). Bema now notices and makes them again.
- Smoother scrolling and slide changes in longer decks.

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
