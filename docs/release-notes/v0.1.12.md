Bema 0.1.12 shows your mouse to the audience.

### Changed
- **The arrow tool shows your pointer on the slides.** While your mouse is over the slide in the presenter window, the audience sees a regular pointer in the same spot, moving with it. Move off the slide and it disappears. (It used to be "Pointer off".)

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
