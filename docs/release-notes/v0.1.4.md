Bema 0.1.4 presents decks you open from OneDrive, SharePoint and Teams.

### New
- **Decks from the cloud**: open a deck in PowerPoint from OneDrive, SharePoint or Teams, then press ⌃⌥P or click **Present** on Bema's welcome screen. Before, Bema said the deck "isn't a saved file on this Mac". Your speaker notes are read through PowerPoint, so a big deck takes a few seconds longer to load, and nothing is downloaded or saved as a copy.

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
