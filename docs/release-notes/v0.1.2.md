Bema 0.1.2 fixes the self view and gives you a switch for macOS's camera effects.

### Fixed
- **Self view turns off and on again reliably.** Closing it could leave Bema thinking it was still open: the camera stayed on and the Self view button did nothing. Closing it now turns the camera off (unless your camera is on the slides), and it always opens again, even with Bema's main window closed.

### New
- **Effects** opens macOS's Video Effects menu for Bema's camera, where you turn Edge Light (the glow around the screen), Portrait or Studio Light on or off. It's in the top bar while the camera is on, in the self view's hover controls and in Stage tools.
- The sample deck (`Samples/Sample.pptx`) is now a designed six-slide talk with notes written to be read aloud.

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
