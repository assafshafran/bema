Bema 0.1.9 makes sure your deck opens in the Bema you're looking at.

### Fixed
- **With two copies of Bema on your Mac,** opening a deck or a `bema://` link could start the other copy, which loaded the deck in a hidden window. A second Bema now hands the deck to the one already open and quits.

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
