Bema 0.1.14 keeps slides without notes in the prompter.

### Changed
- **Slides without speaker notes no longer vanish from the prompter.** The slide keeps its heading with a faint *No notes for this slide* underneath, so you know you're there and can speak freely. It's small and dim so you won't read it out, and Voice never tries to match it.

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
