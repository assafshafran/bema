Bema 0.1.13 fixes the pointer from 0.1.12.

### Fixed
- **The arrow tool's pointer now reaches the audience.** In 0.1.12 the audience only saw your pointer with the laser and other tools. With the arrow tool, your mouse over the slide now shows as a regular pointer on the audience display too.

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
