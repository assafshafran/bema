Bema 0.1.17 fixes a notice that wouldn't go away.

### Fixed
- After starting a show with one display, *Only one display is connected…* stayed on screen even once a second display was plugged in and the show restarted. It now clears when the slides go full screen, and updates as soon as a display is plugged in or out.
- With two displays but the slides on Bema's own display, the notice says so and points to **Slides on**.

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
