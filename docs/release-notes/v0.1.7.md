Bema 0.1.7 writes speaker notes for slides that don't have any.

### New
- **Write notes.** On a slide with no notes, click **Write notes**, or **All slides without notes** for the rest of the deck. They're drafts: edit them, write them again or discard them, and the prompter follows them. **Save to deck** puts them into the deck's speaker notes in PowerPoint or Keynote and saves it. Notes already in your deck are never replaced.
- **Choose who writes them** in **View → Notes Writer Settings…**:
  - **On this Mac** with Apple Intelligence: free, and nothing leaves your Mac.
  - **A local model** in Ollama or LM Studio.
  - **Your Claude or OpenAI API key**: the slide's text and picture go to that company only when you ask for notes. Keys are kept in your Keychain.
  - **The Claude app, with your subscription**: click **Connect Claude Desktop**, reopen Claude, then ask it to write notes for your Bema deck. Bema's button copies the request for you.

### Fixed
- Typing in a text field in Bema no longer moves slides.

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
