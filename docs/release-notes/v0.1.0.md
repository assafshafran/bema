The first release of Bema: a presenter view you control, for PowerPoint and Keynote on Mac.

**Download `Bema.dmg` below**, open it and drag Bema to Applications. macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel, with Microsoft PowerPoint or Apple Keynote.

### What's in it
- **Your own presenter window:** current and next slide, large notes, a filmstrip, and a timer that knows your planned length and tells you if you're behind. Resize it, move it, keep it on top. PowerPoint or Keynote still plays the show on the audience display, full screen.
- **A prompter that follows your voice,** using Apple's speech recognition on your Mac (English and Hebrew tested). It keeps up when you skip ahead or ad-lib. Auto-scroll and manual modes too.
- **A detached prompter that stays out of screen shares,** placed right under your camera.
- **Pointer tools** drawn over the audience slide: laser (five styles), spotlight, magnifier, pen, highlighter, stamps, reactions.
- **Coaching:** pace against a time budget for each slide, words per minute, filler words, and a report after a rehearsal.
- **Phone remote and live polls** over your Wi-Fi, by QR code.
- **Voice commands, live captions, a camera bubble,** three themes and a menu bar mini.
- **⌃⌥P** in PowerPoint or Keynote hands the open deck to Bema. Or right-click a `.pptx` or `.key` in Finder → **Present with Bema**.

### First run
Bema asks to control PowerPoint or Keynote, and for Accessibility so it can put the slideshow full screen on the audience display. The microphone, speech recognition and camera are asked for only when you turn on a feature that uses them.

### Updates
Bema checks for updates once a day (only its version number is sent). You can turn this off in the Bema menu.

### Known limits
- Keynote support is new and has had less live testing than PowerPoint.
- The phone remote and polls need the phone on the same Wi-Fi; some office and guest networks block that.
- Google Slides: download as PowerPoint (.pptx) and present that file.
