Bema 0.1.6 asks where your slides should go, and no longer gets stuck when the show ends in PowerPoint.

### New
- **Choose the display when you start.** With two or more displays connected, Start slideshow asks *Where should the slides go?* Your usual display is already selected, so press Return, or click another one. Untick **Ask every time** to skip the question; turn it back on from **Slides on** in the top bar.

### Fixed
- **Ending the show in PowerPoint** (Esc, or closing its slideshow window), closing the deck or quitting PowerPoint now brings Bema back to your deck with the timer stopped. Before, Bema could stay stuck presenting until you restarted it. If the deck was closed, Start slideshow opens it again.

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
