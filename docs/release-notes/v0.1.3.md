Bema 0.1.3 makes the self view dependable and lets you choose your camera and microphone.

### Fixed
- **The self view closes every time**, from its X, the Self view button, the menu bar or ⌥⌘V. After it had been closed and reopened once, closing it again could do nothing.
- **Self view controls stay visible**: they sit on a dark bar that stays readable over a bright picture (like Edge Light), and in circle mode none are cut off at the edges.

### New
- **Choose your camera** from the self view's camera menu or Stage tools (built-in, an external webcam or your iPhone). The camera bubble on the slides uses it too.
- **Choose your microphone** by clicking the level meter in the prompter. It applies to everything that listens.
- **Make the current slide bigger**: drag the handle under it (double-click to reset).
- **Light** (the sun) opens macOS's Edge Light, Studio Light and Portrait controls, now also in the self view. Pointing at a self view control says what it does.

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
