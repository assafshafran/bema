Bema 0.1.1 brings Bema into the menu bar, a PowerPoint button, your camera, and help.

### New
- **A Present with Bema button in PowerPoint.** Add it from Bema's menu bar menu (or File → Add Bema Button to PowerPoint…), restart PowerPoint, and it's on the Home tab.
- **Self view:** your camera in a small floating window that only you see. It stays out of screen sharing. Open it from the new **Self view** button in the top bar (⌥⌘V).
- **Camera on slides** has its own top-bar button too, next to Self view.
- **Bema lives in the menu bar** and keeps running when you close its window. The menu has **Open at login**, the PowerPoint button, Stage tools and **Check for updates**.
- **Help:** Bema's Help menu opens the new help page at getbema.dev/help, with troubleshooting for the common snags.

### Changed
- **The presenter window is hidden from screen sharing by default,** so a screen share on a call shows your slides, never your notes. Turn it off in Stage tools.
- **Stage tools is easier to find:** a labelled button, Slideshow → Stage Tools… (⇧⌘T), the welcome screen and the menu bar.
- The project moved to github.com/getbema/bema.

### Updating
Bema 0.1.0 offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
